Skills

At Qsoft, we are known for our expertise in technology and commitment to delivering innovative solutions that meet our clients' needs. We pride ourselves on our competence and ability to deliver high-quality, reliable, and scalable products.

Our mass market solutions are designed to meet the needs of a wide range of customers, including website design and development, mobile app development, e-commerce platforms, social media marketing, SEO, PPC advertising, and email marketing. We take a consultative approach, working closely with clients to understand their specific needs, goals and target audience. Our solutions are innovative, reliable and scalable, using the latest technologies and industry best practices to drive business growth and improve operations, helping clients reach a large audience effectively.

Our team is experienced in implementing and maintaining DevOps practices, including CI/CD pipeline, containerization, automated deployment, monitoring and logging, and automated testing. We use tools such as Jenkins, CircleCI, Ansible, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, Prometheus, Grafana, Elasticsearch, Selenium, JUnit and Cucumber to improve the speed, quality, and reliability of software delivery while ensuring security and scalability.

Our digital marketing and SEO services include SEO for organic search results, PPC advertising, social media marketing, content marketing, email marketing and web analytics. Our team uses the latest strategies, tools and techniques to improve search engine ranking, drive website traffic, increase conversions, and achieve client's business goals. We use a data-driven approach to target keywords and use web analytics and tracking tools to monitor and optimize the performance of our campaigns.

Our enterprise solutions include custom software development and implementation of ERP, CRM, SCM, BI and ECM systems. We work closely with clients to understand their specific needs and deliver solutions tailored to meet those requirements, using platforms such as SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, and Microsoft Dynamics among others. Our solutions are designed to automate and streamline business processes, improve efficiency, and provide real-time visibility into key performance indicators. We also provide ongoing support and maintenance to ensure the solutions continue to meet the changing needs of the business and exceed client's expectations.

We offer bespoke business solutions tailored to meet the unique needs of each client. Our consultative approach involves working closely with clients to understand their specific needs, goals, and challenges. Our solutions include a wide range of services such as business process automation, digital transformation, business intelligence and analytics, custom software development, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence and machine learning. We deliver innovative, reliable, and scalable solutions that drive business growth and improve operations, using the latest technologies and industry best practices.

Our government solutions include custom software development, cybersecurity, cloud computing, business intelligence and analytics, digital transformation, IT consulting and support, tailored to meet the specific needs, goals and compliance requirements of government organizations. Our team of experts deliver innovative, reliable and compliant solutions using the latest technologies and industry best practices to improve operations, drive efficiencies and deliver cost savings, exceeding client's expectations.

Our website design and development services include creating responsive and mobile-friendly websites using the latest technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, Ruby on Rails, Angular, Node.js, and others. Our team of experts are skilled in optimizing the website for search engines, ensuring fast loading speed, and providing a user-friendly experience. We work closely with clients to understand their business goals and target audience, and deliver a visually stunning and functional website that meets their specific needs and stand out from the competition. Our web development process includes wireframe, design mockup, development, testing, training and maintenance.

Our mobile solutions include the development of custom mobile apps for iOS and Android platforms using technologies such as React Native, Flutter, Xamarin and others. Our team works closely with clients to understand their needs and goals, and design an intuitive, visually appealing and high-performance mobile app that meets those requirements. We provide ongoing maintenance, updates and ensure that the app is optimized for performance, security and scalability. Our network solutions include design, implementation, and maintenance of enterprise-level network infrastructure using technologies such as Cisco, Juniper, Fortinet and others. Our team of certified professionals provide scalable and secure solutions that meet the specific needs of each client, including new network implementation, upgrades, and troubleshooting. We also specialize in implementing network segmentation, VLANs, VPNs, and other network security measures to protect against cyber threats and ensure compliance with industry standards.
